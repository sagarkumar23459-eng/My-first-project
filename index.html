Some information of school

Aranda High Government School is located in Aranda. It was established in 1955 with the aim of providing quality education to the students of the area. Since its establishment, the school has played an important role in spreading education and knowledge among the local community.

The school focuses on building a strong academic foundation for students. It provides a learning environment where students can grow intellectually and socially. Teachers in the school guide students to develop discipline, curiosity, and a passion for learning.

The vision of Aranda High Government School is to connect students with technology. The school aims to prepare students for the modern world by encouraging the use of technology in education. By doing this, the school helps students develop skills that are important for the future.


our vision

The vision of Aranda High Government School is to use technology for the development of Indian villages. The school aims to inspire students to learn modern technology and use it to solve the problems of rural areas.

About Teachers

The teachers of Aranda High Government School are highly qualified and dedicated. Many of them have been selected through the BPSC (Bihar Public Service Commission) examination.

About Students

The students of Aranda High Government School are very responsible and disciplined. They actively participate in their studies and school activities. Many students are also interested in learning technology and want to connect with the modern digital world.

Dhiru Sir

Diru Sir is considered the heart of Aranda High Government School. He is a dedicated and inspiring teacher who always supports and motivates students. Because of his guidance, many students feel encouraged to work hard and achieve their goals.
